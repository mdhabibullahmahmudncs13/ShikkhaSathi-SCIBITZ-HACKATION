## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

**Step 1: Visit the Platform**
- Open your web browser
- Go to: `http://localhost:5173` (or your platform URL)
- You'll see the homepage with "Get Started" button

**Step 2: Choose Your Role**
Click "Sign Up" and select your role:
- **শিক্ষার্থী (Student)** - If you're a learner
- **শিক্ষক (Teacher)** - If you're an educator
- **অভিভাবক (Parent)** - If you're a guardian

**Step 3: Fill Registration Form**

For **Students**:
```
Full Name: আপনার নাম (Your Name)
Email: your.email@example.com
Password: ******** (strong password)
Grade: Select 6-12
Medium: Bangla/English
```

For **Teachers**:
```
Full Name: শিক্ষকের নাম
Email: teacher@example.com
Password: ********
Subject Specialization: Select subjects
Experience: Years of teaching
```

For **Parents**:
```
Full Name: অভিভাবকের নাম
Email: parent@example.com
Password: ********
Children: Add children's information
```

**Step 4: Verify & Login**
- Check your email for verification (if enabled)
- Click "Login" on homepage
- Enter your email and password
- Click "Sign In"

## 🎓 For Students

### Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll see your dashboard with:

**Top Section:**
- **XP & Level:** Your current experience points and level
- **Streak:** Days of continuous learning
- **Profile:** Your name and avatar

**Main Cards:**
1. **Progress Overview**
   - Completed quizzes this week
   - Total XP earned
   - Current level progress

2. **Recent Activities**
   - Last quiz taken
   - Recent AI tutor conversations
   - Achievements unlocked

3. **Quick Actions**
   - Start AI Tutor Chat
   - Take Practice Quiz
   - View Progress

### Using the AI Tutor

**Starting a Conversation:**
1. Click "AI Tutor" from dashboard or navigation
2. You'll see a chat interface
3. Type your question in the text box
4. Press Enter or click Send

**Example Questions:**
```
"How do I solve quadratic equations?"
"Explain photosynthesis in simple terms"
"What is the Pythagorean theorem?"
"বীজগণিত কি?" (What is algebra?)
```

**Tips for Better Responses:**
- Be specific about your question
- Mention the subject if needed
- Ask follow-up questions
- Use Bengali or English comfortably

**Voice Input (if available):**
1. Click the microphone icon
2. Speak your question clearly
3. AI will transcribe and respond
4. Works in both Bengali and English

### Taking Quizzes

**Step 1: Choose a Quiz**
1. Click "Quiz" in navigation
2. Select a subject:
   - গণিত (Mathematics)
   - বাংলা (Bengali)
   - English
   - পদার্থবিজ্ঞান (Physics)
   - রসায়ন (Chemistry)
   - জীববিজ্ঞান (Biology)
   - ICT

**Step 2: Select Topic**
- Each subject has 3-4 topics
- Choose the topic you want to practice
- Example: Mathematics → Algebra

**Step 3: Configure Quiz**
```
Number of Questions: 5-10
Difficulty: Easy/Medium/Hard
Time Limit: 10-30 minutes
Language: Bengali/English
```

**Step 4: Take the Quiz**
1. Read each question carefully
2. Select your answer (A/B/C/D)
3. Click "Next" to proceed
4. Use "Previous" to review
5. Timer shows remaining time

**Step 5: Submit & Review**
1. Click "Submit Quiz" when done
2. See your score immediately
3. Review correct/incorrect answers
4. Read explanations for each question
5. Earn XP based on performance

### Tracking Your Progress

**Progress Dashboard:**
- **Subject-wise Progress:** See how you're doing in each subject
- **Quiz History:** All quizzes you've taken
- **XP Timeline:** How you've earned XP over time
- **Achievements:** Badges you've unlocked

**Understanding XP & Levels:**
```
Level 1: 0-100 XP
Level 2: 100-300 XP
Level 3: 300-600 XP
Level 4: 600-1000 XP
...and so on
```

**Earning XP:**
- Complete a quiz: 10-100 XP (based on score)
- Daily login: 5 XP
- Maintain streak: 10 XP/day
- Unlock achievement: 50-500 XP
- Perfect quiz score: Bonus XP

**Achievements:**
- 🏆 **Week Warrior:** 7-day streak
- ⚡ **Speed Learner:** Complete quiz quickly
- 🎯 **Perfect Score:** 100% on quiz
- 📚 **Bookworm:** Complete 50 quizzes
- 🌟 **Rising Star:** Reach Level 10

### Maintaining Streaks

**What is a Streak?**
- Number of consecutive days you've learned
- Resets if you miss a day
- Shown on your dashboard

**How to Maintain:**
- Log in daily
- Complete at least one activity
- Take a quiz or chat with AI tutor
- Even 5 minutes counts!

**Streak Benefits:**
- Daily XP bonus
- Special achievements
- Leaderboard ranking
- Motivation to learn consistently

### Offline Learning

**Downloading Content:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Offline Access"
3. Select subjects to download
4. Wait for download to complete
5. Green checkmark when ready

**Using Offline:**
- Access downloaded quizzes
- Review study materials
- View your progress
- No internet needed!

**Syncing:**
- Connect to internet
- Platform auto-syncs your progress
- All offline work is saved
- Continue where you left off

### Creating Assessments

**Step 1: New Assessment**
1. Click "Create Assessment"
2. Fill in details:
```
Title: "Chapter 3 Quiz"
Subject: Mathematics
Topic: Algebra
Grade: 9
Difficulty: Medium
Time Limit: 30 minutes
Total Marks: 100
```

**Step 2: Add Questions**

**Option A: Manual Entry**
```
Question: What is 2 + 2?
Type: Multiple Choice
Options:
  A) 3
  B) 4 ✓ (correct)
  C) 5
  D) 6
Marks: 5
Explanation: Basic addition...
```

**Option B: AI Generation**
1. Click "Generate with AI"
2. Specify topic and difficulty
3. AI creates questions
4. Review and edit as needed

**Option C: Question Bank**
1. Browse existing questions
2. Filter by subject/topic
3. Select questions to add
4. Customize if needed

**Step 3: Review & Publish**
1. Preview the assessment
2. Check all questions
3. Set availability dates
4. Assign to students/classes
5. Click "Publish"

### Grading & Feedback

**Auto-Grading:**
- Multiple choice questions graded automatically
- Instant results for students
- Grade distribution shown
- Class average calculated

**Manual Grading:**
1. Go to "Submissions"
2. Select assessment
3. Click on student submission
4. Review answers
5. Assign marks
6. Provide written feedback
7. Save and publish grades

**Feedback Best Practices:**
- Be specific and constructive
- Highlight strengths
- Suggest improvements
- Encourage effort
- Use Bengali if student prefers

## 🔧 Common Tasks

### Changing Your Password

1. Click on your profile
2. Select "Settings"
3. Click "Change Password"
4. Enter current password
5. Enter new password
6. Confirm new password
7. Click "Update"

### Updating Profile Information

1. Go to Profile
2. Click "Edit Profile"
3. Update information:
   - Name
   - Email
   - Phone number
   - Profile picture
   - Bio
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Enabling Notifications

**Desktop:**
1. Settings → Notifications
2. Enable browser notifications
3. Allow when prompted
4. Choose notification types

**Mobile:**
1. Install as PWA
2. Settings → Notifications
3. Enable push notifications
4. Select preferences

### Downloading the App

**As Progressive Web App:**

**On Mobile (Android/iOS):**
1. Open in browser
2. Click browser menu (⋮)
3. Select "Add to Home Screen"
4. Confirm installation
5. App icon appears on home screen

**On Desktop:**
1. Look for install icon in address bar
2. Click "Install ShikkhaSathi"
3. App opens in its own window
4. Pin to taskbar for quick access

### Troubleshooting Login Issues

**Forgot Password:**
1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check email for reset link
4. Click link and set new password
5. Login with new password

**Account Locked:**
- Wait 15 minutes
- Try again
- Contact support if persists

**Wrong Dashboard:**
- Logout
- Check you're using correct account
- Login with right credentials
- Contact support if role is wrong

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Students

**Learning Tips:**
1. **Be Consistent:** Learn a little every day
2. **Use AI Tutor:** Don't hesitate to ask questions
3. **Review Mistakes:** Learn from incorrect answers
4. **Set Goals:** Daily or weekly targets
5. **Take Breaks:** 25 minutes study, 5 minutes break

**Quiz Strategies:**
1. Read questions carefully
2. Eliminate wrong answers first
3. Don't spend too long on one question
4. Review before submitting
5. Learn from explanations

**Maximizing XP:**
- Complete daily quizzes
- Maintain your streak
- Try different subjects
- Aim for high scores
- Unlock achievements

### For Teachers

**Effective Teaching:**
1. **Regular Assessments:** Test frequently, grade quickly
2. **Use Analytics:** Identify struggling students early
3. **Provide Feedback:** Detailed, constructive comments
4. **Engage Students:** Use gamification features
5. **Communicate:** Keep parents informed

**Time Management:**
- Use AI quiz generation
- Set up auto-grading
- Schedule classes in advance
- Batch similar tasks
- Use templates

**Student Engagement:**
- Recognize achievements
- Create friendly competition
- Vary assessment types
- Make learning fun
- Celebrate progress

### For Parents

**Supporting Learning:**
1. **Check Progress Weekly:** Stay informed
2. **Encourage Consistency:** Help maintain streaks
3. **Celebrate Achievements:** Recognize effort
4. **Communicate with Teachers:** Stay connected
5. **Set Realistic Goals:** Age-appropriate expectations

**Creating Study Environment:**
- Quiet, dedicated space
- Good lighting
- Minimal distractions
- Regular schedule
- Comfortable seating

**Balancing Screen Time:**
- Set reasonable limits
- Encourage breaks
- Mix online and offline learning
- Monitor usage
- Promote other activities
