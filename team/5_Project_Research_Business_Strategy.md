## 💼 Business Strategy Fundamentals {#fundamentals}

### What is Business Strategy?
Business strategy is a comprehensive plan that outlines how a company will achieve its goals, compete in the market, and create value for stakeholders.

#### Core Components
1. **Vision & Mission**: Long-term aspirations and purpose
2. **Market Analysis**: Understanding target customers and competition
3. **Value Proposition**: Unique benefits offered to customers
4. **Revenue Model**: How the business generates income
5. **Competitive Advantage**: What makes the business unique
6. **Growth Strategy**: Plans for scaling and expansion

#### Strategic Framework
```
🎯 Strategic Planning Process
├── Market Research & Analysis
├── Competitive Intelligence
├── Customer Segmentation
├── Value Proposition Design
├── Business Model Canvas
├── Financial Modeling
├── Risk Assessment
└── Implementation Roadmap
```

### Why Strategy Matters for EdTech
- **Market Timing**: Education technology adoption is accelerating
- **Scalability**: Digital platforms can reach millions of students
- **Impact**: Technology can democratize quality education
- **Sustainability**: Proper strategy ensures long-term viability
- **Investment**: Clear strategy attracts funding and partnerships
